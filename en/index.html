How we work

Your visit, step by step

No rush and no obligation. This is what a visit to Anaka Optician feels like.

1

You book an appointment

Book online in a minute, call us or message us on WhatsApp. You choose the day and time.

2

We examine your eyes

A full optometry exam and, if needed, retinography. We explain every result clearly and calmly.

3

We choose together

We advise you on the frame and lenses that best suit your face, your lifestyle and your prescription.

4

We look after you afterwards

Adjustments, check-ups and follow-up whenever you need them. We are here, in the neighbourhood.
